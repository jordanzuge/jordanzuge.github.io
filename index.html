PROJECT — 01 Conceptual Design
500 KW EDGE DATA CENTER
COOLING SYSTEM — PHOENIX, AZ

Conceptual mechanical design of a 500 kW mission-critical cooling system for a Phoenix, Arizona deployment — completed independently to demonstrate applied HVAC system design beyond my naval work. The extreme desert climate (110°F design day) drives every major design decision: air-side economization is impractical in summer, making mechanical cooling with evaporative pre-cooling the optimal strategy. Designed to N+1 redundancy per Uptime Institute Tier II standards and ASHRAE TC 9.9 thermal guidelines.

Design Rationale: Phoenix's extreme summer heat (110°F design day) eliminates air-side economization as a primary strategy — the outdoor air is too hot to provide free cooling. However, the exceptionally low relative humidity (<15% in summer) makes evaporative pre-cooling highly effective, allowing condenser entering air temperature to be reduced by 15–25°F with minimal water consumption. This hybrid approach reduces mechanical cooling demand while maintaining system reliability. Hot aisle containment is implemented to prevent recirculation, maximizing CRAC unit efficiency and maintaining predictable ΔT across all rows.

PROJECT — 02 Senior Design
PRECISION METROLOGY SYSTEM
FOR CNC RIFLING QC

Designed and calibrated a machine vision inspection system for automated quality control of broached rifling grooves in 9mm pistol barrels. Responsible for optical system design — lens selection, working distance calculation, and pixel-to-dimension calibration — enabling sub-millimeter measurement accuracy from a digital image feed. The system marks extremes on each groove edge and classifies barrel cross-sections as pass/fail against pre-defined dimensional tolerances.
